## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-05

First release of GPT Notebook.

Documentation refresh: separate language manuals, real interface screenshots, fictional sample archives and simpler Wiki navigation.

- Local reading of ChatGPT export folders and ZIPs, including branches, Markdown, code, formulas and media.
- Title/message editing, undo/redo, search, turn navigation and seven export routes.
- Snapshot-based saving and net incremental history with summaries and read-only detail previews.
- Eleven interface languages, reading preferences and last-viewed conversation restoration.
- English/Chinese documentation, source build instructions and automated Windows/Ubuntu checks.

Downloads: **gpt-notebook.html** for everyday use; **Source code (zip)** for building and development.
